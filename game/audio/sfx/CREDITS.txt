Fumo Court sound effects
========================

All sounds here are built from Warsong's sound packs for the Touhou fan games
"Petaldust" and "Nostalgiac Blues", released under Creative Commons Zero
(public domain): https://warsong.pages.dev/tags/touhou/

tools/build_sfx.py rebuilds these files from the packs. Which pack sound went
into which slot:

  ui_hover         goodblip            (Nostalgiac Blues)
  ui_confirm       healthrestore       (Nostalgiac Blues)
  ui_cancel        badblip             (Nostalgiac Blues)
  nav              clothC              (Petaldust)
  shout_objection  big damage + slashC (Nostalgiac Blues + Petaldust)
  shout_hold       destroyed + slashB  (Nostalgiac Blues + Petaldust)
  gavel            HeadCup + hurteffect
  stamp            hurteffect + landingsound
  banner           badbell             (Petaldust)
  penalty          breakeffect         (Nostalgiac Blues)
  breakthrough     success             (Petaldust)
  shock            cirnobullet         (Nostalgiac Blues)
  yeet             faraway             (Nostalgiac Blues)
  case_closed      HeadCup + hurteffect + success (low and high)
                   + reconfirm + progress, retuned into one A-major chord,
                   with a synthetic hall reverb
  mistrial         unexpectedoutcome   (Petaldust)
  blip             goodblip, repeated  (Nostalgiac Blues)
