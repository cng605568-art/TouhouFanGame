Music
=====

cirno_theme.ogg
    "Beloved Tomboyish Girl" by +TEK
    from the album "Story of Modulated Frequency" (2017)
    https://plustek.bandcamp.com/track/beloved-tomboyish-girl
    License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
    Changes: cut where the tune repeats, with a 0.12 s crossfade at the cut
    so it loops seamlessly (the rest of the repeat and the fade-out are not
    included),
    leading silence trimmed, loudness-normalised to -17 LUFS and re-encoded
    as Ogg Vorbis from the FLAC download (tools/build_music.py).

    Arrangement of "おてんば恋娘" (Tomboyish Girl in Love), composed by ZUN
    (Team Shanghai Alice) for Touhou Koumakyou ~ the Embodiment of Scarlet
    Devil. Used in this free, non-commercial fan game under the Touhou
    Project fan-work guidelines. No audio is taken from the official games.
