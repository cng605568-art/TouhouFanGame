    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
    Changes: cut where the tune repeats, with a 0.12 s crossfade at the cut

    as Ogg Vorbis from the FLAC download (tools/build_music.py).

    (Team Shanghai Alice) for Touhou Koumakyou ~ the Embodiment of Scarlet
    Devil. Used in this free, non-commercial fan game under the Touhou
    Project fan-work guidelines. No audio is taken from the official games.
